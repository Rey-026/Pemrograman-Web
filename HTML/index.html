About Tokyo

Tokyo is the capital of Japan and one of the most electrifying cities on the planet. With over 37 million people in its greater metropolitan area, it holds the title of the world's most populated urban region. Yet despite its sheer scale, Tokyo feels surprisingly navigable an intricate web of train lines, neighbourhood pockets, and hidden alleyways that rewards the curious traveller at every turn.

The city formally became Japan's capital in 1869, when Emperor Meiji relocated the imperial seat from Kyoto. Since then it has survived two near total destructions the Great Kantō Earthquake of 1923 and the firebombings of World War II and rebuilt itself each time into something even more remarkable. Today's Tokyo is a seamless layering of ancient shrine culture, mid century modernism, and bleeding edge technology.

Why Visit Tokyo?

There is genuinely no other city quite like it. Tokyo manages to be simultaneously ancient and futuristic, loud and serene, overwhelming and deeply orderly. You can eat a Michelin starred ramen in a basement for ¥1,200, then walk ten minutes to a 400 year old Shinto shrine completely hidden behind a convenience store.

🍣

World-Class Food

More Michelin stars than Paris and New York combined. From ¥500 street food to omakase experiences that book out months in advance.

🚆

Perfect Transport

30+ train lines, 900+ stations, and a reputation for trains that arrive within seconds of their scheduled time. Getting lost is almost impossible.

⛩️

Culture & History

Senso-ji temple, Meiji Shrine, and the Imperial Palace sit comfortably beside anime shops, robot restaurants, and digital art museums.

🛡️

Incredibly Safe

Consistently ranked among the safest major cities in the world. Lost wallets are routinely turned in to police boxes with all cash intact.

Best Time to Visit

Tokyo is a year round destination, but each season offers something completely different.

🌸 Spring (Mar–May)

Cherry blossom season transforms parks like Ueno and Shinjuku Gyoen into pink dreamscapes. Book accommodation 3–4 months ahead.

🎆 Summer (Jun–Aug)

Hot and humid, but alive with summer festivals (matsuri), fireworks over the Sumida River, and yukata clad crowds.

🍁 Autumn (Sep–Nov)

Arguably the best season. Crisp air, fiery foliage in temple gardens, no crowds. A photographer's paradise.

🎍 Winter (Dec–Feb)

Illumination festivals light up Roppongi and Marunouchi. New Year shrine visits are a once in a lifetime experience.
