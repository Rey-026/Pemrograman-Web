Planning Your Tokyo Trip?

Here are a few things worth knowing before you go:

  • IC Card — Get a Suica or Pasmo card at the airport. It works on every train, bus, and many convenience store purchases.
  • Pocket WiFi — Rent one at the airport. Having offline Google Maps on your phone is a lifesaver in the Tokyo metro system.
  • Cash — Japan is still heavily cash based. ATMs at 7 Eleven and Japan Post accept foreign cards reliably.
  • Shoes — You will walk 20,000–30,000 steps a day. Wear comfortable shoes. This is not a drill.
