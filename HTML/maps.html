Key Landmarks

These are the spots worth marking on your own map before you land.

🗼 Tokyo Tower

The 333m iron lattice tower modelled after the Eiffel Tower. Best viewed from Roppongi Hills at sunset.

🏯 Imperial Palace

The primary residence of the Emperor of Japan, surrounded by moats and old Edo castle walls. The East Gardens are open to the public.

⛩️ Meiji Shrine

A forested Shinto shrine in the heart of the city. 70 hectares of woodland — a peaceful escape surrounded by the urban sprawl.

🌸 Shinjuku Gyoen

Tokyo's finest park — 58 hectares mixing Japanese, French, and English garden styles. The best place in the city for cherry blossom viewing.

🎡 Odaiba

A futuristic artificial island in Tokyo Bay accessible by the driverless Yurikamome line. Home to teamLab Borderless and a life-size Gundam.

🐟 Tsukiji Outer Market

Even though the inner wholesale market moved to Toyosu, the outer market still thrums with fresh seafood stalls, tamagoyaki, and the best breakfast in Tokyo.

Getting Around

The Tokyo train network is one of the wonders of the modern world. Here's what you need to know:

  • JR Yamanote Line — The green loop line that circles central Tokyo, stopping at all major hubs: Shibuya, Shinjuku, Harajuku, Ueno, Akihabara, and more. Learn this line first.
  • Tokyo Metro & Toei Subway — Nine and four lines respectively, reaching every corner of the city. A Pasmo IC card covers both seamlessly.
  • Taxis — Clean, honest, and expensive. Use them for late nights when trains stop running (midnight–5 am).
  • Walking — Many famous areas (Harajuku → Shibuya, Ginza → Tsukiji) are pleasant 20-minute walks. Don't always default to the train.
